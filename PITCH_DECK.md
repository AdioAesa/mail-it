## Slide 3: The Solution

### **NearRun: Same-Day Hand-Delivered Cards**

A gig-economy platform that connects people who want to send heartfelt cards with local "Mailers" who hand-deliver them.

**How It Works:**

1. **Choose** a beautiful card template or upload your own
2. **Write** your personal message
3. **Add** optional extras (flowers, chocolates, gift cards)
4. **Send** - a local Mailer hand-delivers it same-day

**The Magic:** A real person delivers a real card, with a real smile.
