The Problem

Digital Killed the Greeting Card

  • 6 billion greeting cards sent annually in the US, but the industry is declining
  • E-cards feel impersonal and forgettable
  • Traditional mail takes 3-5 days and often arrives late
  • Nobody remembers the last e-card they received
  • People want connection, but convenience wins
I wanted to send my mom flowers for her birthday across the country, but I forgot until the last day and couldn't find anything personal enough.

The Solution

Same-Day Hand-Delivered Cards

A gig-economy platform that connects people who want to send heartfelt cards with local "Mailers" who hand-deliver them.

How It Works

1 Choose a beautiful card template or upload your own
2 Write your personal message
3 Add optional extras — flowers, chocolates, gift cards
4 Send — a local Mailer hand-delivers it same-day

The Magic: A real person delivers a real card, with a real smile.
